# Product

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
People who think with both writing and files — researchers, builders, designers, students — who want one calm desktop workspace where notes, folders, media, and spatial thinking stay rooted in their own filesystem.

Primary job: open a folder, write in markdown, browse and preview real files, and arrange references on a canvas without importing or duplicating anything.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, local-first.

## References
- Obsidian — markdown-as-files, folder-native notebooks
- Linear — dense but quiet app chrome, keyboard-first navigation
- Arc — restrained spatial chrome without dashboard clutter
- Refern — file-forward collecting without forcing a proprietary vault

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS note apps that import/sync everything into a cloud vault
- Loud productivity dashboards with cards, stats, and purple gradients
- File explorers that treat writing as an afterthought
- Whiteboards that detach from the real filesystem

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast for text, visible focus rings, keyboard shortcuts for core navigation, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
